# BinaHub public website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Executives, managers, HR teams and founders explore BinaHub development solutions,
read their scope and request a conversation. Mobile browsing is a primary use case.
These facts and the compact, simple catalog direction are confirmed by the user.

## Constraints

- Preserve the CEO's catalog content; do not invent or shorten the underlying scope.
- Indonesian page headings and interface on the Indonesian route; catalog item
  titles retain the original English names. English routes retain English UI.
- Public catalog never exposes internal prices or operational approval language.
- Only published, ready, non-mock products/modules come from the existing API.
- Catalog selection and contact handoff remain available; diagnosis is a separate journey.

## Product principles

- Make scanning, searching and comparing easy on a phone.
- Use progressive disclosure for full details, not deletion of important information.
- Preserve keyboard access, clear feedback, and recovery from loading failures.
